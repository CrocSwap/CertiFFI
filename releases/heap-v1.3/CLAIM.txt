For the 14 Heap families (worked normal and CT, plus 6 initial/trap and 6 trap-CT), the certified entry certiffi_heapv2_shim in heap-v2-d163.elf (sha256 032b5400...) satisfies its C1 boundary theorems over the canonical GB10 configuration source, on standard GB10 Linux, with no debugger, tracer or hardware profiler attached to the process. This holds for every admissible configuration completion, including MTE2 present or absent, under the premises in the ratified manifest fea4a431; premises needing deployment-time attestation are marked as such. The binary is to be loaded as an ordinary static PIE per C7 (no relocations). The caller must pass 32-byte output and input buffers (x0, x1) that, together with the 64 bytes immediately below SP, are pairwise disjoint and do not wrap the address space, with SP at least 64 and SP, x0 and x1 16-byte aligned. At deployment, the tracked buffer pages and the shim's 16-byte save slot must not be mapped twice (no double mmap). This is a deployment obligation: it is not proved, and it holds only if attested at the actual deployment. The theorems assume the memory system returns status OK for the run's tag reads (PhysMemTagRead, Sail choice site 1354), an unattested hardware obligation. Generic-timer state is per-entry data, and the timers are assumed quiet over the execution window (GB10.TimersQuiet). From the certified entry, execution never leaves the reviewed instructions except to stop at the reviewed trap word or return to the caller. The rest of the binary (the _start harness, an unreachable memmove) and the rest of the process are not verified. Every premise set is shown jointly satisfiable at every admissible completion (positive C2). Constant time (CT0, security grades v2.1): for each of the 7 CT families, two executions on the same reviewed path whose public inputs (buffer addresses and lengths) agree run to the same final step with equal model event records, and every event of every step of both runs has a record. For the worked CT family the final step is the return to the caller; for the 6 trap-CT families it is CT0 to the trap: the run ends at the abort (certiffi_trap), not a return. Both runs are taken under one caller source configuration; pairs of runs under different caller configurations are not covered. The event records are the model's explicit accesses by virtual address and size, fetch and table-walk traffic by physical address and size, translation markers and ISB, with no data values; this is a property of the Arm architecture model and says nothing about caches, TLBs or prefetchers. The only proved instance of the two-run premises is a pair of identical runs (identical-run non-vacuity only): no pair of admissible runs with different secrets has been exhibited. No secret residue in general-purpose registers (CT2.R0): at the final step of each of the 7 CT families, every general-purpose register is either equal across the two runs or the run's own entry value. On the worked CT family, x0-x8 and x10-x17 are zeroed by the body, x18-x30 are returned unchanged (the caller's own values), and x9 holds the caller's DIT image (the full MRS DIT value, per run). On the 6 trap-CT families the run ends at the abort before the body writes the remaining registers: x0, x1 and x30 are equal across runs, x9 holds the caller's DIT image, and every other register is returned unchanged. SP is equal across runs. This inherits the identical-run non-vacuity of CT0. NZCV, memory written during the run and SIMD/SVE state are not graded. Hardware timing independence for data-processing instructions additionally requires PSTATE.DIT = 1 and Arm's Data Independent Timing guarantee for DIT-listed instructions (claim condition P3, not modelled and not measured); the shim's DIT theorem shows, under its hypotheses, DIT = 1 throughout the body and DIT restored at exit. The CT1 grade (data-independent timing) is not claimed. Speculative-execution side channels, including straight-line speculation (SLS), are not addressed: the image is not built with SLS hardening, and no speculation property is claimed. All CT grades are about the architectural (Sail) semantics.
